# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this list useful.

## Rules

- One tool per entry, on a single line.
- Format: `` - [Name](https://link) — one-line description. ``
- Alphabetical within each section (Tartarus and other **hosted** flagship
  entries may lead their section).
- The link must work and point at the project's home (repo or site).
- Open source or self-hostable, unless tagged **(hosted)**.
- No affiliate links, no dead projects, no duplicates.

## How

1. Fork, add your entry in the right section.
2. Keep the description factual; no marketing superlatives.
3. Open a PR explaining what the tool does and why it belongs here.
